

$X \sim \operatorname{Bernoulli}(p)$

$X = 0$ with probability $p$ or $X = 1$ with probability $1-p$

$X = \mathbb{1}(\text{event})$ , i.e., 0 or 1. Function $\mathbb{1}$ is called indicator function.

$p = P(\text{event}) = P(X=1)$

$\operatorname{E}(X) = 0 \cdot (1-p) + 1 \cdot p = p$

$\operatorname{Var}(X) = p(1-p)$

[[Memory is Something We Do]] 
* [[verify analytically]] that maximum value of variance is $1/4$.

> [!example]- solution
> plot $p(1-p)$
> Variance has maximum value when $p=1/2$: $p(1-p)=1/4$
> $\operatorname{Var}(X) \le 1/4$ for any $p$
