The **absolute Monte Carlo error**  (needs exact value $\theta=E[g(X)]$):
$$
\left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}g(x_i)-E[g(X)]\right|
$$
or the corresponding relative version, the [[relative Monte Carlo error]],

$$
\frac{\left|\frac{1}{n}\sum_{i=1}^{n}g(x_i)-E[g(X)]\right|}
{|E[g(X)]|},
$$
give an ideia of the quality of simulation and analytical results.

The relative Monte Carlo is used to compare several simulations.
