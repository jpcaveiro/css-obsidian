# Monte Carlo methods for univariate distributions

* The term "Monte Carlo" (MC) is a reference to the casino of Monte Carlo (Roulette tables are random number generators in a sense).

Generically, Monte Carlo methods are all computational tools where one **examines properties of a probability distribution**, generating a large sample of a given distribution and then the sample statistical properties are studied.


In this section we will consider several Monte Carlo Methods in order to numerically approximate the mean value $E[g(X)]$ for a real $g$ function.
