Markov Chain Monte Carlo methods:

Monte Carlo simulation methods via Markov Chains (MCMC) constitute a class of algorithms **to simulate complex multivariate distributions**. They are based on the construction of a homogeneous Markov chain whose distribution is the limit of the distribution to be simulated. The Metropolis-Hastings algorithm and, in particular, the Gibbs are some of these methods.
