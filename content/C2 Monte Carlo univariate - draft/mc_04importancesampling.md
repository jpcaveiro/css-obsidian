A **heavy tail** is a distribution whose probabilities decrease slowly for extreme values, so unusually large or small observations are more common than in a normal or exponential distribution. It often leads to many outliers and may make the mean or variance unstable or even infinite. [en.wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heavy-tailed_distribution)
